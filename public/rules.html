Community rules

Everyone here is a fellow student. Keep it that way.

  • Be kind. No harassment, hate, threats, or bullying.
  • Keep it clothed. No nudity or sexual content, ever.
  • No recording. Don't record or screenshot anyone without their consent.
  • Keep personal info private. Don't share anyone else's details, and think twice before sharing your own.
  • Your account is yours. Don't let anyone outside ASTU use it.
  • Uncomfortable? Press Next. You never owe anyone a conversation.
